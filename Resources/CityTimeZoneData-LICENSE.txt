Derived by retaining city ID, name, normalized multilingual aliases, country code, administrative division code/name and IANA time-zone identifier; records compressed with LZFSE. Aliases use Foundation case/diacritic folding (en_US_POSIX). Coordinates, population and other fields were removed.
The source covers cities with more than 15,000 inhabitants and capitals; it is not an exhaustive list of every locality. Data is provided as-is without warranty of accuracy, timeliness or completeness.
